# Product

<!-- impeccable:product-schema 1 -->

## Platform

web

## Stack

Static HTML/CSS generated by a zero-dependency Node script (`skill/scripts/build-site.mjs`) from the markdown learning journal. No framework, no build step beyond the script, no client JS dependencies. Must work opened from `file://` and hosted under a GitHub Pages subpath (all links relative). Deploy target: GitHub Pages first; any static host (e.g. Vercel) works.

## Users

Primary: the learner, re-reading and reviewing their own lessons. Anyone from a non-technical "vibe coder" to a senior engineer who builds software with AI through Claude Code and the socratic-builder skill. They open it to recall what they learned, see what's still fuzzy, and notice whether their confidence matches reality.

Secondary: people the learner chooses to share it with (hiring managers, peers) when published as a portfolio of how they learn.

## Product Purpose

Turns an append-only markdown learning journal into a readable, searchable site, so lessons get re-read instead of forgotten. Success: the learner returns to it; published, it shows honest evidence of learning over time.

## Positioning

Each entry records the *before* ("I thought"), the *after* ("Actually"), a practical check, and the learner's own one-sentence explanation, plus spaced-review history and confidence ratings. It's a record of changed understanding, not a list of links or notes.

## Operating Context

Entries are drafted automatically by Claude during building sessions; the learner adds their "In my words" sentence. Spaced-review questions resurface entries at 1, 7, 30 and 90 days. The site is rebuilt on demand with `/socratic-site` and published only by explicit choice.

## Capabilities and Constraints

- Pages: index (search, tag filter, list of lessons), one page per entry, and a calibration view (confidence vs correct from review history).
- Drafts and entries marked `private` are never included in the site.
- Content may be in plain English or technical language depending on the learner's profile.
- Must be print-friendly and support light and dark.
- No external dependencies except optional web fonts.

## Evidence on Hand

No real entries yet (the author's journal is empty). Sample content for development must be clearly marked as sample and never shipped as a user's own record.

## Product Principles

1. Honest over impressive: show gaps, drafts-in-progress counts and misses as plainly as wins.
2. Reading first: the learner's own words are the hero content.
3. Private by default: nothing leaves the machine without an explicit publish step.
4. Plain by default: understandable to a non-technical reader.
